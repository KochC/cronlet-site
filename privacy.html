Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cronlet does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app requires no account and makes no network connections of its own.

Data collection

Data Not Collected. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, no tracking.

On-device data

Your jobs (shortcut names, schedules, optional input text) and the run history are stored only on your device in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Cronlet schedules local notifications on your device to trigger your shortcuts. They are created and delivered entirely on-device; no push server is involved.

Purchases

The optional Cronlet Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer receives no personal or payment information.
